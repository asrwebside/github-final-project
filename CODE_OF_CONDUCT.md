# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Showing empathy and respect.
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards and taking appropriate corrective action when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions when someone represents the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may issue a warning, temporarily suspend participation, or permanently remove a contributor depending on the severity and circumstances of the violation.
